HourSet — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026

HourSet does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There is no account to create, no server to sign in to, and the app makes no network requests of its own.

What the app stores

Everything HourSet knows about you is written to your own device and stays there:

  • your training plan and the days in it
  • the sets you log — weight, reps and time
  • your settings: units, equipment available to you, injuries or exercises you

have marked as not for you

This is held in the app's private storage. No copy is sent anywhere. Uninstall the app and all of it is deleted with it.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry or usage tracking
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No accounts, sign-in or email collection
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data

Permissions

HourSet requests one permission: notifications. It is used only to show your rest timer on the lock screen while a workout is running, so you do not have to keep the app open. It is optional — decline it and the app works normally with the timer on screen.

Spoken cues

If you turn on spoken cues, HourSet passes the name of the exercise and the cue to your device's own text-to-speech engine. On most Android phones that is Google Text-to-Speech, which may process the phrase on-device or on Google's servers depending on your own system settings. HourSet sends it nothing else, and no information about you is included.

Backups

If you have Android Backup switched on for your device, Android may include HourSet's stored data in your own device backup. That is handled by Android under Google's terms, not by HourSet, and you control it in your device settings.

Children

HourSet is a general-purpose fitness app and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, of any age.
