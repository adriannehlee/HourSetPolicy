HourSet — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

HourSet has no account and no sign-in. It collects nothing about you. Your training stays on your own device.

There are two exceptions. Both are worth stating at the top rather than burying.

The subscription screen talks to a payment service. It fetches prices, and checks whether you have already paid. If you never open that screen, the app never contacts anybody about money. What it sends is set out under "Purchases".

If the app hits an error, it sends the error. Not what you were doing, and not what you lifted. It sends the fault itself, where in the code it happened, and the phone model and operating system version. No identifier goes with it. Two reports from the same phone cannot be recognised as coming from the same phone. This is set out under "Crash diagnostics".

One other thing can leave your device. If you switch spoken cues on, the cue goes to your phone's own text-to-speech engine. That is described further down.

What the app stores

Everything HourSet knows about you is written to your own device and stays there:

  • your training plan and the days in it
  • the sets you log: weight, reps and time
  • your settings: units, equipment available to you, injuries or exercises you have marked as not for you

This is held in the app's private storage. No copy is sent anywhere. Uninstall the app and all of it is deleted with it.

None of this is ever transmitted, including when you buy a subscription. Your training history is not part of a purchase. It is never sent with one.

Purchases

HourSet is free for 7 days. After that it needs a subscription or a one-time purchase. Payment is handled by Apple or Google. HourSet never sees your card, your billing address or your store account.

To know whether you have paid, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service operated by RevenueCat, Inc. in the United States. When it runs, it sends:

  • a random identifier RevenueCat generates for this installation. Not your name, email, or any account of yours, and not linked to anything else
  • the purchase receipt issued by Apple or Google
  • your device platform, operating system version, app version and country

When it runs is deliberately limited. The app does not start RevenueCat when it launches. It contacts the service in two situations:

  • When the subscription screen is shown. During your free week that only happens if you tap the countdown or open it from Settings. Once the free week has ended, that screen is the app, so it happens every time you open it.
  • When a subscription you already have is within 2 days of renewing, to check it is still valid. This is the only thing that happens without you opening a screen. It never applies to anyone who has not subscribed.

Two consequences worth stating plainly. If you never open the subscription screen, HourSet makes no network requests at all. That is the whole of it, not a qualified version. And the one-time lifetime purchase cannot lapse, so it is never re-checked.

RevenueCat's own privacy policy is at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/.

Crash diagnostics

When something in the app goes wrong, a report is sent to Sentry (https://sentry.io), a diagnostics service. It contains the error, the line of code it came from, and the device model and OS version. That is the whole of it.

What it deliberately does not contain:

  • No identifier. No account, no device id, no advertising id, no installation id. Reports arrive unattributable, so nobody can build a picture of one person from several of them.
  • No IP address stored. The app attaches none. Sentry is configured to discard the one its servers see when the report arrives.
  • No release health or session pings. Sentry can report every launch and foreground of the app, with an installation id attached. That is switched off. Nothing is sent unless something has gone wrong.
  • No screenshots. Sentry can attach a picture of the screen at the moment of an error. On this app that would be a picture of your workout. It is off.
  • No record of what you did. Sentry can log every tap and screen change alongside an error. That is switched off. A trail through the app is a log of your workout under another name.
  • No performance monitoring. That is telemetry about ordinary use rather than about a fault. This app does not do telemetry.

An error inside the app does not register as a crash with Apple or Google. Without these reports, a fault could only be reported as "it broke". A bug nobody can locate is a bug that stays.

Sentry's privacy policy is at https://sentry.io/privacy/.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry or usage tracking. The crash reports above are faults, and are sent only when one happens
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No accounts, sign-in or email collection
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access
  • No profiling, and no selling or sharing of anything with anybody
  • No third-party SDK that collects data, other than RevenueCat and Sentry as described above. One answers "has this person paid". The other answers "what went wrong"

Permissions

HourSet requests one permission: notifications. It is used only to show your rest timer on the lock screen while a workout runs. You do not have to keep the app open. It is optional. Decline it and the app works normally, with the timer on screen.

Spoken cues

Turn on spoken cues and HourSet passes the exercise name and the cue to your phone's own text-to-speech engine. On most Android phones that is Google Text-to-Speech. It may process the phrase on the device, or on Google's servers. Your own system settings decide which. HourSet sends it nothing else. No information about you is included.

Backups

Android Backup is your device's own setting. With it on, Android may include HourSet's stored data in that backup. That is handled by Android under Google's terms, not by HourSet. You control it in your device settings.

Your rights over purchase data

The identifier RevenueCat holds is random. It is not tied to your name or email. That means we cannot look you up from your own details. To have the record for your installation deleted, email the address below. Send it from the account you bought with, and we will pass the request on. Deleting it does not cancel or refund a subscription. That is done in the App Store or Google Play.

Your training data needs no request. It is on your phone. Uninstalling deletes it.

Children

HourSet is a general-purpose fitness app. It is not directed at children under 13. It collects nothing that identifies anyone, of any age. It collects nothing at all about anyone who does not open the subscription screen or hit an error.
