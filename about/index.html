HourSet — What it is

Last updated: 3 October 2026

HourSet is a strength-training app for iPhone and Android that plans your gym session every day. It is made for people who are new to lifting weights, or coming back to it, and want to be told what to do.

What it does

  • Plans each session for you. Open the app and today's workout is already there: the exercises, the order, the sets and the rest between them.
  • Trains what has recovered. HourSet keeps track of what each muscle has had and how recently. Each session trains what is ready, and within a few sessions it has covered the whole body.
  • Fits the time you have. You choose how long a session lasts. The warm-up and cool-down are included in that time.
  • Uses only your equipment. Say what your gym has, or that you train with dumbbells only or your bodyweight. Exercises that need anything else are left out.
  • Works around what hurts. Mark a joint or a muscle and exercises that load it are left out.
  • Shows every exercise. More than 200 exercises, each with a drawing of the movement and plain instructions.

Who it is for

Adults who have a gym membership and do not know what to do there, people returning to lifting after a break, and people losing weight who want to keep their muscle while they do. It is not a coach and does not know your medical history.

Privacy

  • No account. There is no sign-up, no email and no password.
  • Your training stays on your phone. The plan and every set you log are stored on the device and never uploaded.
  • Anonymous usage counts only. Counts such as "a workout was finished", with no identifier, which can be switched off in Settings. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

Price

Browsing the plan is free. Starting a workout needs a subscription. In the United States it costs $9.99 a month or $29.99 a year, with the first year at $19.99 for new subscribers. Prices in other countries are set by the App Store and Google Play and shown in local currency before you pay.

There is no free trial that turns into a charge. With notifications on, a yearly plan gets a reminder a week before it renews. Cancel any time in the App Store or Google Play.

Where to get it

  • Android: HourSet on Google Play, package com.hourset.app.
  • iPhone: search for HourSet: Full Body Gym Workout on the App Store.
