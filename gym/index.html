HourSet

A free week of HourSet

A strength-training app that plans workouts for the equipment in your gym and the time you have. Every exercise drawn, with steps to follow.

Free for 7 days, then the monthly price shown in the store, until you cancel. New subscribers only.

On an iPhone

  1. Get the free week. Open the App Store
  2. Tap Redeem, then install HourSet.
  3. Open HourSet and set it up. If HourSet asks you to subscribe, tap Restore a purchase.

On an Android phone

Google Play needs a payment method, even for the free week.

  1. Copy the free-week code. HOURSETGYM
  2. Install HourSet. Open Google Play
  3. Open HourSet and set it up. Tap Confirm workout, then Start workout. Come back to this page.
  4. Pick Monthly, not Yearly. Tap Subscribe, not Have a code?
  5. In Google's payment screen, tap the arrow beside your payment method, then Redeem.
  6. Paste the code. Check the free week is listed, then confirm.

How do I cancel?

Cancel at least a day before the free week ends and you pay nothing.
