HourSet — Support

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Cancel

In the App Store or Google Play, not in HourSet. The store takes the payment, so the store stops it.

In HourSet, Settings → Subscription → Manage subscription opens the right page.

On an iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → HourSet → Cancel Subscription.

On Android: Google Play → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → HourSet → Cancel subscription.

Get a refund

On an iPhone: go to reportaproblem.apple.com. Sign in with the Apple Account that paid, then pick the purchase.

On Android: follow Google's steps at Get refunds on Google Play.

Get your purchase back

Open Settings, the gear at the top right of the main screen. Under Subscription, tap Restore a purchase.

Use the Apple Account (iPhone) or Google Account (Android) that paid. A purchase belongs to the account, not to the phone. If the app says No earlier purchase found, you are signed in with a different account.

Report a wrong drawing or inaccurate instructions

Email adriannehlee@gmail.com with:

  • the exercise name
  • what is wrong: which limb, which position or which step, and what it should be
  • a screenshot

Report anything else

Email adriannehlee@gmail.com with:

  • what you were doing when it happened
  • your phone model and its iOS or Android version
  • which version of HourSet you have, from its App Store or Google Play page

If today has no workout, or a short one

If it says today is a rest day, part of your body is still recovering. The screen says when your next workout can start. Tap Train anyway to build one as if everything had recovered.

If the workout is still empty or short, three rows in Settings, under Your workouts, leave exercises out. Each row shows how many things are in it.

  • Equipment. Exercises that need anything turned off here are left out.
  • Injuries or pain. Exercises that work these hard are left out. A joint leaves out every exercise that uses it.
  • Not for me. Exercises you have turned down. This row appears once you turn one down.

Turn things back on and today's workout is rebuilt.

If the lists are short, check Workout length at the top of Settings. A short length leaves room for fewer exercises.

Change your equipment

Settings → Equipment.

Pick a preset if one matches your gym: Full gym, Home gym, Dumbbells only or Bodyweight only. To fine-tune it, tap Choose specific equipment and turn off anything your gym does not have. Only the equipment you have gets used.

Leave an exercise out

On the main screen, tap How to do it on the exercise. Turn on Not for me. It leaves today's workout and every one after.

To change it for today only, tap Swap on the exercise before you tap Confirm workout. That picks another exercise for the same movement.

To put one back: Settings → Not for me.

If you delete the app

Uninstalling deletes your plan, your logged sets, and your settings. If you'd like to keep your logged sets and settings, make sure to export them in Settings → Your history → Export history.

To clear the history and keep the app, use Settings → Your history → Delete history. Every logged set is deleted, with your weight suggestions and exercise level progress. Your plan and settings stay.

What the app collects

Nothing about you. The privacy policy sets out what leaves your phone: the purchase check, crash reports, and anonymous usage counts, which you can turn off in Settings → Privacy.
